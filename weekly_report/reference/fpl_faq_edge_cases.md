# FPL edge cases — from the official FAQ

Source: https://fantasy.premierleague.com/en/help/faqs (retrieved 2026-08-26). The situations the rules page doesn't spell out but that decide rounds. Load when one of them happened.

## Double and blank gameweeks

- A player whose club plays twice scores in both matches. A captain is doubled (or tripled) in both.
- **Autosubs only trigger on zero minutes across the whole gameweek.** A player who plays one of two matches is not substituted for the other; a captain who plays one of two is not replaced for the other.
- A club with no fixture scores nothing that gameweek. The rescheduled match scores for whoever owns the player *when it is played*, not when it was originally scheduled.

## Chips

- **Triple Captain on a captain who doesn't play**: the ×3 passes to the vice-captain. If the vice doesn't play either, the chip is spent and not returned.
- **Free Hit**: at the next deadline squad and bank return to what they were at the previous deadline, with that week's price changes applied. Any bank change made during the Free Hit week is lost.
- Wildcard and Free Hit use up that gameweek's free transfer; transfers saved from earlier gameweeks are kept.
- A Wildcard is not an unlimited budget — the manager stays within their current squad value plus bank.
- A Wildcard shows as *Pending* (and can still be cancelled) until a second transfer is confirmed; then it is *Active* and locked. Free Hit is locked the moment transfers are confirmed. Confirmed transfers can never be reversed.
- Only Bench Boost and Triple Captain can be cancelled before the deadline, and only they are available in a manager's first gameweek.

## Minutes and the 60-minute rule

- FPL minutes exclude stoppage time. The 60th minute runs 59:00–59:59, so a player substituted "on 60" has played 59 → loses the second appearance point **and** clean-sheet eligibility.
- An outfield player forced to go in goal collects save points but scores as his own position (a midfielder in goal gets 1 for a clean sheet, not 4).

## Squads and real-world transfers

- A real transfer between PL clubs can leave a manager with four from one club. The squad stands as it is; the manager must get back under three at their next transfer.
- A player who leaves the Premier League stays in the squad scoring **0** until the manager spends a transfer on him.
- New signings appear in the game within 7 days of the transfer completing.

## Numbers Reidar may quote

- **Form** = a player's average points per match over his club's matches in the last 30 days.
- **FDR** (fixture difficulty, 1–5) comes from Opta data plus each club's home/away form over the last six matches; reviewed weekly.
- Defensive-contribution and bonus points move with Opta's post-match review until the gameweek is marked final (09:00 UK, day after the last match). A "lost" 2-pt DC or a bonus swing after full time is normal.

## Tie-breaks

- Classic league: equal points → fewer transfers (Wildcard/Free Hit transfers excluded) ranks higher; still equal → the position is shared.
