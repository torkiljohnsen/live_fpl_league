## The two games in this league

Managers here play two games at once: **overall rank** over 38 weeks, and the **golden gameweek** cash prize every 4th gameweek (4, 8, 12 … 36). The two pull in different directions, and a manager's chip timing reveals which game they're playing.

## Chip timing

- **Triple Captain**: for overall rank, play it in a double gameweek on a premium with two good fixtures. For the golden-gameweek cash, save it for a GGW — even a single-fixture TC on the right player can win a 10-manager round.
- **Bench Boost**: needs a full squad of starters, so it fits right after a rebuild — GW1 (unlimited transfers before the first deadline) or the week after a Wildcard. Best value in a double gameweek where the bench plays twice.
- **Free Hit**: for blank gameweeks (few teams playing) or a huge double. Cannot follow a Free Hit in the previous gameweek.
- **Wildcard**: the first one is usually best spent when the early-season picture has settled (GW4–8) or when injuries/price drops have wrecked the squad; the second when the fixture swings for the run-in are known. A Wildcard immediately followed by a Bench Boost is the classic combo — and a tell: if someone wildcards the week before a GGW, expect a Bench Boost.
- Both halves of the season have a full set of chips; unused first-half chips die at the GW19 deadline. A manager sitting on all four chips in GW17 is either planning something or asleep.

## Transfers and hits

- A −4 needs the incoming player to out-score the outgoing by more than 4 over the weeks you keep him. Chasing last week's points rarely clears that bar.
- Saved free transfers (up to 5) are worth more than most single moves; rolling into a big fixture swing beats a weekly tinker.
- The template protects your rank; differentials move it. Which one a manager wants depends on which of the two games they're playing.

## Reading a manager's intentions

- Wildcard played → look for Bench Boost next week, especially before a GGW.
- All chips used by GW15 → they're playing for cash, not rank.
- Rolling transfers into a GGW → planning a hit-free double move.
