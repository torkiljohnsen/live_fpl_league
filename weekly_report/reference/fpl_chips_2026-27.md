# FPL chips 2026/27

Source: https://fantasy.premierleague.com/en/help/rules (retrieved 2026-08-26). Chip availability windows are also in the API: `bootstrap-static.chips[].start_event / stop_event`.

One chip per gameweek. Every chip comes in **two sets**: one for each half of the season, split at the **GW19 deadline (Sat 2 Jan 14:30 UK)**. Unused first-half chips are lost.

| Chip | Effect | First set | Second set | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Wildcard** | All transfers that gameweek are free, including ones already made | after GW1 until GW19 deadline | after GW19 deadline to season end | Played when confirming transfers; cannot be cancelled |
| **Free Hit** | Unlimited free transfers for one gameweek; squad reverts at the next deadline | after GW1 until GW19 deadline | after GW19 deadline | Cannot be played in consecutive gameweeks; cannot be cancelled |
| **Bench Boost** | Bench points count | from season start until GW19 deadline | after GW19 deadline | Set on Pick Team; can be cancelled before the deadline |
| **Triple Captain** | Captain ×3 instead of ×2 | from season start until GW19 deadline | after GW19 deadline | Set on Pick Team; can be cancelled before the deadline. If the captain doesn't play, the vice-captain is tripled |

- Saved free transfers survive a Wildcard or Free Hit (2 saved before → 2 saved after).
- Bench Boost and Triple Captain are the only chips available in GW1 (Wildcard and Free Hit open *after* the first gameweek).
- Wildcard/Free Hit transfers don't count towards the "fewest transfers" tie-break.
