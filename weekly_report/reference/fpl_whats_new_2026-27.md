# What's new in FPL 2026/27

Source: https://fantasy.premierleague.com/en/help/new (retrieved 2026-08-26). Only the changes that affect scoring or how a gameweek plays out; onboarding/autopick/UI tweaks omitted.

- **Points lock at 09:00 UK the day after the last match** (was one hour after the final whistle). Opta's six-hour post-match review now feeds into BPS and defensive-contribution points before the lock. Practical effect: a gameweek stays provisional for a day or more; late bonus/DC changes are normal, not errors.
- **Ranks and league tables update in real time** during the gameweek, and live totals include provisional bonus points (shown from ~20 minutes into a match).
- **BPS rebalanced** to reduce the overlap between defensive-contribution points and bonus:
  - clearances/blocks/interceptions now give 1 BPS per **3** (was per 2) → centre-backs slightly less likely to take bonus on a clean sheet, full-backs and keepers more likely
  - the −1 BPS for *being tackled* is removed → attacking full-backs, wingers and flair players gain
  - goalkeepers: 2 BPS per save, +1 if from inside the box, +1 if from a Big Chance; saves from outside the box no longer a separate metric; penalty save 8 → 7 BPS (it always counts as a Big Chance too)
- **Price changes**: daily deadline fixed at **midnight UK**. A new Price Change page shows each player's progress towards a rise/fall and a "likely / very likely" status — price moves are now predictable in-game.
- **New views** on Pick Team / Points / Transfers: fixture difficulty (next three), ownership with trend, 30-day form.
- **Rookie League**: first-season managers are auto-entered into a global league of their own.
